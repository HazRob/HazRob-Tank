### T3-023 — FINAL APPROVED user R0 sequence (2026-10-20)
Status: OWNER APPROVED. Overrides the earlier proposed stabilization extension, rejection-on-drift, and further approval pending language.

- 00:00–05:00: sensor supply ON; initial fixed warm-up, no averaging of samples.
- 05:00–08:00: measurement window #1, read all eight channels simultaneously in software from sequential ADC scans.
- 08:00–11:00: ON/pause #1, do not include readings in R0 computation.
- 11:00–14:00: measurement window #2.
- 14:00–17:00: ON/pause #2, do not include readings in R0 computation.
- 17:00–20:00: measurement window #3.
- 20:00: per-channel field R0 calculation and final **'حفظ قيم R0'** dialog; previous saved values stay active until the operator confirms saving.

Simplification required by owner:
- Do NOT switch sensors off during the pauses. Do NOT automatically extend the 5-minute warm-up; do NOT add per-sensor instability qualification, drift thresholds or forced recapture. Use only baseline validity checks to prevent divide-by-zero, missing data and fabricated values.
- Every channel's new R0 derives from its own three capture windows. Preserve the existing field-baseline geometric aggregation: per-window geometric mean of positive valid Rs values in kΩ, then equal weighting of the three window means in log space. All channels captured for the same fixed duration; no calibrated reference gas required.
- Show current and calculated R0 for MQ2, MQ3, MQ4, MQ5, MQ7, MQ8, MQ9, MQ135. If one channel has no valid readings, report **'تعذر احتساب R0'** for it and preserve its existing value; do not block the valid channels from saving.
- Single explicit final save. Store time, source, and accepted values; after saving, invalidate/reload live per-channel normalization cache and re-run *future* ChemCore with current accepted per-device R0. Prior saved analytics remain frozen; restore old values on cancellation or failed transaction.
- Firmware currently has fixed per-channel R0 constants; Android/ChemCore must use newly saved app-side values, not unverified embedded firmware ratios. Runtime propagation must be tested before any V3 delivery.
- Field user calibration is operational normalization only, NOT a manufacturer/reference-gas R0 or verified ppm. MQ7/MQ9 heater waveform and RL assumption remain hardware limitations; do not silently label them laboratory-certified.
- This is a design decision recorded for V3, not evidence of an implementation or approved APK release.
