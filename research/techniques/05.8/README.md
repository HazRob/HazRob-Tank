# 05.8 LOD/LOQ — HazRob Tank

**PARTIAL_PROTOTYPE — screening only, not formal LOD/LOQ.** Estimates the sample mean and sample standard deviation of >=10 independently sampled blank signals for one specified gas/sensor protocol, and computes one-sided heuristic signal thresholds at 3× and 10× blank SD in a declared increasing/decreasing response direction. A measured signal is classified as below screening detection, between screening detection and quantitation, or above screening quantitation threshold. The code **always returns quantifiable=False** because exceeding a signal threshold does not establish concentration accuracy, bias, precision, selectivity or traceability. Thresholds remain in the original signal units; never report them as ppm without independently validated inverse calibration and uncertainty.

Blank readings must be genuinely independent under a controlled blank protocol; serially correlated readings and environmental fluctuations can invalidate the sigma estimate. For rigorous method detection/quantitation limits, characterize low-level standards, blank distributions and false-positive/false-negative rates, calibration slope/nonlinearity, replicate accuracy/precision, humidity/temperature, sensor drift, mixtures, cross-sensitivity and per-gas selectivity. Standards such as ISO 11843 / IUPAC definitions may require a different decision rule; no compliance is claimed. A generic MQ response cannot prove a specific gas is present. No safety alarm decisions.
